About Roomly

Find a campus room with less guesswork.

Roomly helps University of Michigan-Dearborn students move from a campus map to a building floorplan, then narrow in on rooms that fit their study plans.

Campus First

Start with the buildings students already know and choose a location from the map.

Floor by Floor

Move through one level at a time with clear floor controls and readable keyplans.

Built to Grow

Room overlays and reservation details can expand as more campus data is added.
